# Tanmay’s Portfolio

## Purpose

Give recruiters and engineers a quick way to see what Tanmay built, why he made key decisions, and where to inspect the work.

## Visitor experience

- Open the home URL to a closed book cover with the positioning **Backend & Applied AI Engineer** and an **Open portfolio** button.
- Show two pages on desktop with previous and next buttons and arrow-key navigation. Show one page at a time on mobile with swipe and tap controls.
- Use chapter bookmarks for About, Projects, Research, Experiments, and Resume. Give each spread a shareable URL. Direct URLs open the spread immediately, and browser Back and Forward restore the corresponding view.
- Animate page turns about the spine and respect reduced motion. Keep content, links, and media controls usable throughout normal navigation.

| Chapter | Content |
| --- | --- |

| Projects | Vendor AI Assistant and OAK-D-Lite Pothole Detection |
| Research | Meta-Contrast with the method, Tanmay’s contribution, and verified results |
| Experiments | OpenClaw work and interview builds |
| Resume | View or download `Tanmay_Patel_Resume.pdf` when supplied, with a QR pointing to the same public destination once available |

For featured spreads, pair the problem, contribution, and engineering decision with supporting media or results. Put approved code, videos, screenshots, paper, thesis, and Colab links alongside the work. Use the supplied `assets/` folder as input. Omit missing links and verify metrics against their sources.

Deliver the cover, responsive navigation, chapter spreads, and available evidence. Show **Ask how I built this** collapsed and labeled as a preview. If opened, explain that live chat is coming later. Verify navigation on desktop and mobile, individual spread URLs, and available assets.
